# Draft Public-Records Request — Texas Missing Foster-Youth Safeguards

**Status:** Draft template; not submitted.  
**Date:** 2026-11-13  
**Subject:** De-identified records concerning children/youth missing from DFPS conservatorship, required notifications, search activity, court exits while missing, and trafficking safeguards.

## Request scope

Under the Texas Public Information Act, request existing public records maintained by the Texas Department of Family and Protective Services (DFPS) concerning children/youth reported missing from DFPS conservatorship for FY2019–FY2025. This request is intentionally limited to aggregate, de-identified, and administrative/process records. It does **not** request names, dates of birth, addresses, schools, photographs, placement addresses, case numbers, medical records, or other information that could identify a child.

If responsive records contain protected or confidential child information, please redact or withhold the protected fields and release reasonably segregable non-confidential data. If DFPS cannot release de-identified episode-level data, provide existing aggregate tables and data dictionaries.

## Records requested

1. **Annual population and outcome data:** For each FY2019–FY2025, counts of unique children and missing episodes; children already missing at start of fiscal year; new missing episodes; repeat missing episodes; located/recovered during year; still missing at year-end; exited conservatorship while missing; turned 18 while missing; court-terminated conservatorship while under 18 and missing; deceased while missing. State the exact definitions, cohort rules, and denominator for every measure.
2. **FY2023 reconciliation:** Explain and provide supporting aggregate records for 1,164 unique children missing at some point, 1,016 located by August 31, 2023, 109 still missing in conservatorship on that date, 39 exits while missing (15 aged out, 23 court-terminated under 18, one deceased), and 1,095 children recovered during FY2023. Reconcile whether the 1,095 recovery count is from the same cohort as the 1,164 missing population.
3. **FY2024 reconciliation:** Explain the population/definitions behind the reported 935 children who went missing, 919 recovered, and 192 suspected/unconfirmed and/or confirmed trafficking cases. Provide end-of-year unresolved count and dispositions, if maintained.
4. **Notification compliance:** Aggregate counts and elapsed-time distributions for notice to NCMEC, law enforcement, parents/guardians, attorneys ad litem, courts, placement providers, and tribes where applicable. Provide the applicable policies, procedures, checklists, and time requirements in effect for each year.
5. **Search and recovery activity:** Existing aggregate reports on caseworker searches, supervisor escalation, high-risk response, repeat-missing response, law-enforcement referrals, interagency coordination, and post-return interviews/services. Include performance measures and quality assurance reviews.
6. **Court exits while missing:** Aggregate counts by fiscal year of petitions and orders terminating DFPS conservatorship while the child remained missing, separated by children under 18 and youth who reached 18. Provide non-identifying policy guidance, approval criteria, legal review standards, training, and quality assurance procedures governing these petitions. Provide aggregate outcomes and reasons where maintained.
7. **Trafficking safeguards:** Aggregate counts of screening/assessment completion, suspected/unconfirmed vs confirmed sex/labor trafficking, victimization identified after recovery, and referrals/services provided. Preserve separate suspected and confirmed categories.
8. **Data integrity:** Data dictionaries, system field definitions, missing-episode entry/edit/deletion rules, duplicate resolution logic, audit-log retention schedules, system reconciliation reports, and quality assurance findings. Provide aggregate counts of records corrected, deleted, merged, or found incomplete.
9. **Contracts and provider accountability:** Policies, provider contract provisions, incident reporting standards, and aggregate monitoring records relevant to placement providers' notification and cooperation when a child goes missing. Do not disclose placement addresses or identify children.
10. **Federal reporting:** Aggregate reconciliation of DFPS missing episodes to NCMEC reports and federal reporting submissions, including late, missing, rejected, or corrected reports.

## Preferred format

- CSV/XLSX for tabular data; PDF for policies, reports, audits, and manuals.
- Include field definitions, extraction date, reporting period, system of record, and calculation methodology.
- Stable random non-identifying IDs may be used to distinguish repeated episodes, provided they cannot be reversed to case numbers or identities.
- Do not provide any child-identifying key or crosswalk.
