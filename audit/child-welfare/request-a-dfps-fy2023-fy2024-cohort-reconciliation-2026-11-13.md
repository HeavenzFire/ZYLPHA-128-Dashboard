## Request text

Under the Texas Public Information Act, Texas Government Code Chapter 552, I request copies of existing public records maintained by the Texas Department of Family and Protective Services (DFPS) that document the definitions, source tables, calculations, and quality-control procedures used to report children missing from DFPS conservatorship in FY2023 and FY2024.

This request is limited to existing aggregate, de-identified, and administrative records. I do not seek names, dates of birth, addresses, schools, photographs, placement locations, case numbers, medical information, or any other individually identifying child information.

Please provide the following records, if maintained:

1. The existing source tables, spreadsheets, report extracts, or other records used to produce the published FY2023 missing-child and recovery totals.
2. The existing source tables, spreadsheets, report extracts, or other records used to produce the published FY2024 missing-child and recovery totals.
3. The data dictionary, field definitions, report specifications, cohort rules, and written methodology applicable to those totals.
4. Existing records that define and distinguish:
   - unique children versus missing episodes;
   - children already missing at the beginning of a fiscal year;
   - new missing episodes and repeat episodes;
   - recoveries during the fiscal year versus recovery of a specific fiscal-year cohort;
   - children still missing at fiscal year-end;
   - children who exited conservatorship while missing, separated into age-out, court-ordered termination before age 18, and death.
5. Existing records reconciling the FY2023 published figures: 1,164 unique children/youth missing at some point during the year; 1,016 located as of August 31, 2023; 109 still missing and in DFPS conservatorship on that date; 39 exits while missing (15 aged out, 23 court-terminated under age 18, and one deceased); and 1,095 unique children recovered during FY2023.
6. Existing records reconciling the FY2024 published figures: 935 children who went missing, 919 reported as recovered, and 192 cases categorized as suspected/unconfirmed and/or confirmed trafficking.
7. Existing quality-assurance, data-validation, or reconciliation reports concerning the completeness of missing episodes, duplicate or repeat episodes, recoveries, end-of-year status, or exits while missing.

For each record or data extract, please include its reporting period, creation or extraction date, system of record, field definitions, and calculation methodology if those details are already part of the record.

Please provide records electronically in their existing native format where available (CSV/XLSX for tabular records and PDF for documents). If an existing record contains confidential or protected child information, please redact those fields and release reasonably segregable nonconfidential portions. Please do not create a new analysis or dataset solely to answer this request. If a requested record is not maintained, please state that it is not maintained.

If the scope is considered too broad, please contact me with specific narrowing options and process the records concerning the FY2023/FY2024 source tables, cohort definitions, and year-end unresolved/exit dispositions first.

Please provide an itemized written cost estimate before incurring charges above [insert authorized amount]. If any portion is withheld, please identify the specific statutory basis asserted and release all reasonably segregable nonconfidential portions.

Thank you.
