# Targeted Records-Request Drafts

These are templates, not submitted requests. Identify the correct component, check public reading rooms first, and insert record IDs or dates before filing. FOIA generally covers existing agency records; it does not generally require creation of new analysis. Exemptions, fees, and deadlines apply.

## Request A — OIG recommendation tracking records
**Recipient:** Relevant agency Inspector General FOIA office, identified through Oversight.gov.  
**Subject:** FOIA request — Inspector General recommendation tracking records, 2010–2026

Pursuant to FOIA, I request existing electronic records sufficient to identify recommendations issued from January 1, 2010, through December 31, 2026, including recommendation identifier, report identifier, date issued, recommendation text, agency response, priority designation, status history, closure date, and documented basis for closure or a determination that the recommendation was no longer valid.

Please provide existing CSV, XLSX, or equivalent machine-readable records if maintained in that form. If the entire period would be burdensome, advise whether annual batches or an existing export are available. Identify fields not maintained or withheld, state the applicable legal basis for withholding, and release reasonably segregable non-exempt portions. Please notify me before fees exceed: [insert threshold].

## Request D — FOIA processing data or case-specific determinations
First use FOIA.gov's published annual/quarterly reports and agency reading rooms. If those do not answer a specific question, request existing records for defined fiscal years or identified request numbers, such as processing-time reports, disposition records, and determination letters. Avoid requesting unrelated requesters' personal information.

## Filing and tracking checklist
- Confirm the likely records custodian.
- Search reading rooms and public datasets first.
- Identify specific records, IDs, program, and date range.
- Specify electronic format where available.
- Set a fee threshold and use the agency's official submission portal.
- Save the request, receipt, tracking ID, correspondence, determination, redactions, and appeal deadlines.
- Do not treat delay, denial, or a record not located as proof of wrongdoing.
