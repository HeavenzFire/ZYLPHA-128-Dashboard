# Federal Audit Workstreams (2010–2026)

## WS-01 — Oversight recommendations (first priority)
Questions: What recommendations were issued, to whom, and when? Which remain open, were implemented, closed without implementation, or invalidated? What evidence supports closure? Which are overdue under the source's own criteria?

Sources: GAO recommendation records and priority reports; Oversight.gov/CIGIE and individual OIG reports; agency corrective-action plans and public responses.

Required fields: source system, recommendation ID, report ID, agency, issue date, recommendation text, priority, status, status effective date, closure basis, implementation evidence, follow-up report, source URL.

Control: do not add GAO and OIG totals; populations and status definitions differ.

## WS-02 — Spending, procurement, grants, and financial controls
Questions: Do award, obligation, outlay, modification, and recipient records reconcile? Are questioned costs or financial-control weaknesses documented? Were corrective actions subsequently tested?

Sources: USAspending.gov, agency award records, GAO financial audits, OIG audits, agency financial statements.

Required fields: award/transaction ID, agency, recipient, period, amount type, modification history, audit finding ID, questioned costs where documented, agency response, disposition.

Control: improper payments are not synonymous with fraud; an award does not prove delivery, waste, or misconduct.

## WS-03 — FOIA transparency and records access
Questions: How do receipts, completions, backlog, processing times, and dispositions vary by agency/year? What is already public? What legal grounds were cited for denials or redactions?

Sources: FOIA.gov annual/quarterly datasets, agency reports and reading rooms, determination letters lawfully obtained.

Required fields: agency/component, fiscal year, received, processed, pending at start/end, time metrics where reported, disposition, exemptions where known, dataset version.

Control: volume/backlog alone does not establish unlawful withholding or intent. Document schema changes.

## WS-04 — Regulatory accountability
Questions: What legal authority and rationale did the agency state? What was proposed, finalized, amended, or rescinded, and when? What public record supports the action?

Sources: Federal Register, historical eCFR versions, statutes, public agency dockets, GAO/OIG reports.

Required fields: document/docket ID, agency, statutory authority, CFR citation, publication/effective dates, version, public comments/response records, source URL.

Control: use the rule version effective on the relevant date; a discrepancy alone is not a legal conclusion.

## WS-05 — Information-system controls
Questions: What control deficiency did an authorized auditor document? Was it remediated and independently validated? What scope and period were assessed?

Sources: GAO FISCAM, public GAO/OIG IT and cybersecurity audit reports, agency public remediation statements, authorized audit reports.

Required fields: public system identifier where applicable, control objective, report ID, assessment period, source-defined severity, remediation owner/date, validation evidence, status date.

Control: do not test government systems without explicit authorization. Use public reports or approved engagements.

## Cross-workstream correlation
Link records through agency, fiscal year, program, award, report ID, and recommendation ID. Correlation is a lead, not proof of causation. Record alternative explanations and contradictory records.

## Deliverables
1. Source registry and retrieval log.
2. Normalized datasets and data dictionaries.
3. Deduplication and reconciliation notes.
4. Finding register with evidence references and status labels.
5. Targeted FOIA requests for documented public-record gaps.
6. Final report with methods, findings, limitations, agency responses, and reproducible calculations.
