# VA Community Care Network — public-records request package (DRAFT)

**Status:** Draft only. Not submitted.  
**Prepared:** 2026-11-13  
**Audit reference:** VA OIG Report 23-00748-28; GitHub Issue #9  
**Purpose:** Obtain the contract instruments, audit-closure evidence, and financial recovery records needed to determine whether and how VA corrected or recovered the dental reimbursements described by the OIG.

## 1. Scope and safeguards

This request seeks existing agency records concerning the VA Community Care Network (CCN) Optum contracts:

- Region 1: PIID 36C79119D0004
- Region 2: PIID 36C79119D0005
- Region 3: PIID 36C79119D0006

Relevant period: March 1, 2021 through the date of search, with earlier contract documents included where needed to establish original rates.

**Do not provide veteran names, Social Security numbers, dates of birth, addresses, medical records, or other personally identifying or protected health information.** If source systems contain claim-level identifiers, please provide de-identified records or aggregate data sufficient to reproduce the financial reconciliation. Please segregate and release all reasonably segregable non-exempt portions.

Request electronic production in native format where practicable (PDF for executed instruments; CSV/XLSX for tabular ledgers and rate schedules; EML/PST or searchable PDF for correspondence). Please preserve field names, data dictionaries, and source-system export metadata.

## 2. Request A — Contract file and modification instruments
**Suggested recipient:** VA Office of Acquisition, Logistics, and Construction / Strategic Acquisition Center (SAC), through the VA FOIA channel.

Please provide the complete contract file, including all executed base contracts, modifications, attachments, incorporated schedules, and modification histories for PIIDs 36C79119D0004, 36C79119D0005, and 36C79119D0006, limited to records relevant to dental CDT codes, dental fee schedules, reimbursement rates, and the modification process.

Specifically request:

1. The Optum memorandum/submission delivered in or about March 2021 that included the VHA reasonable-charges dental schedule, including every attachment and version of the schedule.
2. The original contracted dental CDT code/rate schedules in effect immediately before the March 2021 submission.
3. The independent government cost estimate (IGCE), price analysis, negotiation memorandum, contracting officer determination, legal review, approval routing, and supporting workpapers for the 2021 dental-code modification(s).
4. Every signed modification issued in 2021 that added or changed dental CDT codes or rates, including the full instrument, all attachments, effective date, signatures, and distribution/implementation instructions.
5. Every signed May 2023 modification intended to restore existing-code rates to the original contracted rates or otherwise correct the dental fee schedule. Include all three regions and identify the exact modification number for each. Region 1 P00118 and the May 24, 2023 Region 2/3 CDT update entries are search leads only; please confirm or correct them from the official contract files.
6. Any subsequent modification, amendment, bilateral agreement, credit, or corrective action affecting the same dental codes/rates through the date of search.
7. Internal contracting-office communications and review records documenting the discovery of the error, the scope of affected codes, the decision to correct rates, and the implementation of corrected rates in the payment systems.

Search terms may include: “23-00748-28”, “Optum”, “CDT”, “dental fee schedule”, “reasonable charges”, “existing codes”, “new codes”, “66 percent”, “P00118”, “36C79119D0004”, “36C79119D0005”, and “36C79119D0006”.

## 3. Request B — Recommendation 6 recovery and closure records
**Suggested recipients:** VA OALC/SAC, Veterans Health Administration / Office of Integrated Veteran Care (IVC), and VA OIG for records each office maintains.

Please provide all records created or received in connection with VA OIG Recommendation 6 in Report 23-00748-28, which recommended that IVC and OALC collaborate to explore potential recovery of dental payments to Optum.

Specifically request:

1. The complete Recommendation 6 closure/implementation package submitted to and accepted by VA OIG, including the closure memorandum, evidence attachments, approval, and OIG closure determination dated November 26, 2025.
2. The quarterly written updates that IVC said it would request from OALC, and all responses, status reports, briefings, or tracking entries concerning recovery.
3. Any demand letters, notices of overpayment, requests for repayment, negotiations, settlement agreements, disputes, appeals, or correspondence with Optum concerning the dental modification overpayments.
4. The recovery calculation and reconciliation by contract, region, CDT code, date range, and payment cohort.
5. The accounting records showing amounts demanded, acknowledged, disputed, credited, refunded, offset against invoices, recovered in cash, waived, written off, or still unresolved. Identify transaction dates, amounts, accounting references, and the receiving or paying entity.
6. Any credit memos, remittance advices, offset notices, refunds, repayment schedules, journal entries, or other source records supporting amounts reported as recovered.
7. Any legal, fiscal, or contracting determination explaining why any amount was not pursued, was reduced, was waived, or was deemed not recoverable.
8. Recommendation 5 records concerning the development, approval, testing, and implementation of controls over contract modifications, including any evidence submitted to OIG for closure if closure has since occurred.

Please distinguish records about the dental modification finding from records about the separate outpatient healthcare rate-overpayment finding under Recommendation 2. Do not combine the two populations in a single recovery total.

## 4. Request C — De-identified financial reconciliation data

Please provide an electronic, de-identified extract or existing reports sufficient to reconcile dental reimbursements for Optum Regions 1–3 from June 1, 2021 through May 31, 2024, including, to the extent maintained:

- region and contract PIID;
- service/claim date and payment date (date precision may be reduced if necessary);
- CDT procedure code and code effective date;

## 7. Evidence notes — do not overstate

The VA OIG report states that its analysis attributed **$648,662,846** in payments above previously contracted dental rates to the Optum Regions 1–3 modification error. The VA OIG report page lists Recommendation 6 as closed on November 26, 2025, but the publicly available closure status does not state an amount recovered for that dental finding.

The OIG separately reported approximately $14 million recovered from an estimated $105.1 million in Optum outpatient healthcare rate overpayments. That is a different finding/population and must not be counted as recovery of the dental modification overpayment.

Secondary contract-history pages list May 24, 2023 dental/CDT update entries for all three Optum regions. Those entries are leads, not substitutes for signed contract instruments.
