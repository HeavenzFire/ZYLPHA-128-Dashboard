# Claim Verification Status Contract

- VERIFIED: the exact operational definition, cohort, period, unit, and numerator are reproducible from disclosed source artifacts with adequate provenance and completeness evidence.
- PARTIALLY_VERIFIED: some components reproduce, but a material definition, denominator, scope, or limitation is omitted or unresolved.
- CONFLICTED: authoritative sources report incompatible values for the same defined measure, cohort, and period after methodology reconciliation.
- UNSUPPORTED: the necessary underlying measure is absent from the available public sources after a documented search. This is not proof the claim is false.
- NOT_TESTABLE_FROM_PUBLIC_DATA: the claim requires non-public person-level linkage, protected operational records, or other data not lawfully/publicly available.

A difference between incompatible measures is not a conflict. A missing public field is not proof of a negative outcome. A statistical sample estimate must carry its uncertainty and may not be treated as a census count.
